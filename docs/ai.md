# Working with an AI

**English** · [Français](ai.fr.md)

The AI is optional: copy a prompt for the assistant of your choice, or connect your ChatGPT plan (desktop app), OpenRouter (your own key), a local or remote OpenAI-compatible server, or the instance’s AI. To build a diagram from a text, and keep the text beside it, see [Source texts](source-texts.md). The answer formats are described in [The JSON diagram format](map-format.md).

You can use your own AI without connecting anything, or connect one. All these features are also in the menu of the ✦ icon, at the top right of the canvas while editing (a dot marks pending review remarks).

Relation labels (“supports”, “objects to”…) are written in the language of the content (the map's `lang`, kept in the document), whatever the interface language, as long as the relation has not been renamed.

- **☰ menu › AI › “Create or change the diagram with AI…”**: pick a task (create a diagram, enrich, write the sequence, other change), describe it, optionally focus on the selection. The prompt contains the diagram, the vocabulary (including the user's presets, with their definitions), the answer format and the faithfulness rules (never invent a quotation or a source).
  - With no AI connected: “Copy the prompt”, paste it into the assistant of your choice, then paste its answer into the same dialog.
  - With an AI connected: “Ask the AI”; the answer streams in (“Stop” interrupts it). If it fails the checks, it is sent back once to the model with the problems found. It is then shown, with its summary, to be checked and applied.
  - An answer cut by the provider or the network, or nothing received for 90 s: the request is retried once, from the start, then the error is reported. While the model thinks, the length of its thinking is shown. An answer that reaches the token limit is refused (it would be truncated).
  - **Rich diagram** (checkbox of “Create a diagram”, recommended for a rich diagram or after a failure): the AI first proposes a **plan** (`unveilboard/plan` format: the root, the sections, what each develops, its size, and a common structure), which can be reviewed, edited (texts, briefs, order, sections added or removed) or redone with remarks. Each section is then developed on its own, three at a time (synthesis sections after the others, seeing their content), with light thinking; a failed section can be retried alone, without losing the others. Finally, the AI adds the links between sections and the sequence. The resulting diagram (or a partial one, after a stop) is shown to be checked, then opened.
  - **Build it in the diagram** (at the plan step): the skeleton (the root and the section heads) opens as a new diagram, and the plan is kept in the document. The “Diagram plan” panel (✦ menu › “Diagram plan…”) lists the sections and their state (to develop, suggestions to review, developed): “Develop” a section, with its editable brief and a precision for this pass; the answer comes as **suggestions** under its head, to accept or reject (or directly, as an option). “Complete” a developed section adds what is missing. “Develop the remaining sections” runs them in parallel (as suggestions, synthesis sections wait until the others are accepted, to see them). “Links and sequence” finishes the diagram from the accepted elements. Head texts edited on the canvas prevail over the plan's.
  - **Plan for an existing diagram** (✦ menu › “Structure with a plan…”, when the diagram has no plan yet): the AI proposes a plan that keeps the root and the existing branches as sections when they fit (marked “already in the diagram”) and adds the missing ones, with what remains to develop in each. Once reviewed (edited or redone), “Adopt this plan” adds the missing heads (as one undoable change) and keeps the plan: the panel then develops or completes each section, as above.
- **☰ menu › AI › “Critical review…”**: a panel, left of the diagram.
  - **Automatic checks**, free and without AI: an objection without answer, a thesis without justification, an argument-map element attached without a relation, an element the sequence shows before its parent, a box too long.
  - **“Review with the AI”** (or copy the prompt, then paste the answer): the AI reviews the diagram as a demanding teacher would and returns a review in the “unveilboard/review” format ([JSON Schema](review-format.schema.json)): an overall judgement, then typed remarks (inconsistency, gap, confusion, type, relation, structure, redundancy, wording, sequence, faithfulness), with a priority, the elements concerned and, when it is clear, a correction (change operations). An invalid correction is sent back once to the model; after that, the remark stays without correction.
  - Each remark has a number (“R1”) shown at the corner of its elements; clicking an element selects and frames it. “Apply the correction” checks it again on the diagram as it now is, then applies it (undoable); “Dismiss” removes it. The AI's remarks are kept on this device, for this diagram (neither in the document nor in shares).
- **Box and note**: the prompt asks for an explicit, self-sufficient box (a complete claim, not a title), concise, and, if the “Details in the element’s note” option is checked (the default), a brief note when it adds a precision, never the essential alone. Unchecked: everything in the box.
- **Model's thinking** (AI settings): the model's default, none, light, medium or deep. Sent as `reasoning` to OpenRouter (and to the instance's AI, which then adds headroom to the answer's token budget), as `reasoning_effort` to other OpenAI-compatible servers. More thinking gives sharper answers, but slower and dearer ones. Transcription always runs without thinking.
- **Create a diagram from a text**: see [Source texts](source-texts.md).
- **☰ menu › AI › “AI settings…”**: the provider, kept on this device (never in the document or on the server):
  - **Your ChatGPT plan** ([desktop app](desktop.md) only): “Continue with ChatGPT” signs in with your ChatGPT account in your browser; AI requests then use your ChatGPT plan or credits, with no API key, with the models your plan offers (few on ChatGPT Go). It requires a paid plan: with a free plan, ChatGPT stops the sign-in and offers to upgrade. Usage and limits are managed in ChatGPT (“Manage usage”, [chatgpt.com/settings/usage](https://chatgpt.com/settings/usage)); a usage limit stops requests until you raise it or it resets. The sign-in stays on this computer, encrypted by the system (Keychain on macOS), and never reaches the pages: the app's main process sends the requests (OpenAI's Responses API). OpenAI offers this to open-source apps that run on the user's computer, which is why it isn't available on the website;
  - **OpenRouter, with your key**: “Sign in with OpenRouter” creates a key for Unveilboard (OAuth PKCE: the key is exchanged in the browser), or paste your own. The browser calls OpenRouter directly;
  - **OpenAI-compatible server**, local or remote (Ollama, LM Studio, llama.cpp…): address (up to `/v1`), optional key, model (“List the models”). The browser calls it directly: the server must accept the site's origin (Ollama: `OLLAMA_ORIGINS`, and a large enough context, `num_ctx`; LM Studio: enable CORS);
  - **the instance's AI**, if it has one (server-side key, see `.env.example`);
  - “Test” checks that the model answers, and in JSON;
  - **your key** stays in this browser and goes only to the provider: for the tab's lifetime, or on this device if “Remember the key” is checked; signing out of a cloud instance clears it. A flaw in the site, or anyone using the same computer, could still use it: create a key just for Unveilboard, with a spending limit and an expiry date (OpenRouter: *Settings › API Keys*), and don't remember it on a shared computer. More in [SECURITY.md](../SECURITY.md).
- **From an element**: select a box, then “✦ AI…” in its toolbar. A ready-made request (arguments, objections, answers, examples, assumptions, distinctions, definitions, consequences) or your own; with the whole diagram, or only the element, its ancestors and its branch. The AI connects new elements to it (and, if needed, links to existing elements), each with a sentence saying why (`rationale`):
  - **as suggestions** (default): dimmed shapes, labelled “suggestion”, with ✓ and ✕; accepting an element also accepts its suggested parent; rejecting an element rejects its branch. A bar counts the pending suggestions, accepts or rejects them all, and shows why the selected one is suggested. Until accepted, suggestions stay out of the presentation, the JSON export, “Reveal the map”, the handout and shares;
  - **or directly**, as one undoable change.
- **“Sequence (AI)…”** (a tree's toolbar) opens the prompt on writing the sequence, with the order of “Reveal the map” as a starting point.
- **Content language** (AI settings, and “Language: …” in the creation windows, for one creation): the language the AI writes a new diagram in. **Automatic** (the default): the language of the request or of the source text, whatever the interface language, so a French subject gives a French diagram in an English interface. Or the interface language, or any language (a list, or a language code). The diagram keeps its language (`lang`): later requests on it are answered in that language. For a language the app has no labels in (German, Spanish…), the AI also names the relations it uses in that language (`vocabulary` entries with the id of a known relation, e.g. `supports` → “stützt”): the arrows show these names, and the document keeps them for later changes, prompts and JSON export.
- **Prompt library** (✦ menu or ☰ menu › AI › “Prompt library…”): a **method** to follow for a task, chosen in each AI dialog (“Method”: creating a diagram, from a text or in steps, enrich, other change, sequence, from an element, review, plan). It guides what the AI produces (an analysis to carry out, a shape of diagram, the vocabulary to use); Unveilboard still adds its own instructions on the format, so a method never describes the JSON. The last method chosen is remembered for each task, on this device.
  - **Shared collections**: the prompts of the [`prompts/`](../prompts/README.md) folder of the repository, one collection per folder (for instance *Philosophie › Dissertation (bac)*: a subject's conceptual network, analysis of its form, variants of a subject, subjects on one or more notions, a simple essay plan where each part states its role in the discussion; *Philosophie › Explication de texte (bac)*: the argumentative structure of a text, with a sequence written as a line-by-line explanation, its concepts and oppositions, its difficulties). Prompts that work on a text are offered only when the request uses the page's source text, and essay-question prompts only without one. Show only those you need; by default, the collections in the interface language.
  - **My prompts**: write your own, or copy a shared one to adapt it; export and import them as `.md` files. They are kept with your settings: in this browser in local mode, on the server in cloud mode (so on all your devices).
  - **One prompt, several tasks**: the text is sent with every task where the prompt is offered; a line such as `## @create` or `## @expand @enrich` starts a variant sent only for those tasks. The passes of a creation in steps (plan, sections, links and sequence) follow the variant for creation, and a plan kept in the document remembers its method for later sections. **To develop an existing branch** with a method, select its box, then “✦ AI…” in its toolbar (or ✦ › “Develop the selected element…”), and choose the method: the written request becomes optional.
- **Models**: the catalogue of OpenRouter models offered (and allowed for the instance's AI) is in [`src/lib/ai/models.json`](../src/lib/ai/models.json), free to edit; its `default` (DeepSeek V4.1 flash) is used when no model is chosen.
- **☰ menu › File › “Paste JSON (diagram or changes)…”**: a whole diagram opens as a new diagram; changes apply to the open diagram. The JSON may be surrounded by text or a code block, as in an AI's answer.
- **For an agent that operates the browser**, the page exposes `window.unveilboard`:
  - `getMap()`: the open diagram, in this format;
  - `getPrompt(task, instruction?)`: the full prompt for a task (`create`, `enrich`, `sequence`, `review`, `edit`, `expand`; `review` answers with a review, which `apply` shows in the review panel);
  - `apply(json)`: applies changes, or opens a whole diagram as a new one; returns `{ ok, kind, problems }`, with problems in English, so the agent can correct its answer.

**The instance's AI** (route `/api/ai`): the server receives the task, the diagram and the vocabulary, builds the prompt itself and relays the provider's answer; it is not a generic chat relay. In cloud mode it is reserved to the session; in local mode (public instance), only with `AI_PUBLIC=on`, with per-IP limits.

The prompts are in [`src/lib/ai/prompts.ts`](../src/lib/ai/prompts.ts), versioned (`PROMPT_VERSION`).
